## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a grounded, atmospheric dark fantasy aesthetic tailored for a high-utility dragon-rental registry and guild administration interface (*Sistem Rental Naga*). The visual identity balances **80% administrative utility** with **20% atmospheric fantasy storytelling**. Rather than adopting flashy video game heads-up displays, neon gradients, or translucent glass layers, the aesthetic draws inspiration from ancient stone-carved archival ledgers, volcanic obsidian, weathered leather scrolls, and antique forged brass instruments.

### Core Character & Emotional Resonance
- **Archival & Grounded:** Evokes the gravity of an old-world registry bureau managing massive, volatile mythical creatures with meticulous clerical care.
- **Utilitarian Discipline:** Dense, functional, and scannable. Data grids, inspection logs, and rental contracts take center stage without visual clutter or whimsical distractions.
- **Warm Volcanic Contrast:** Deep charcoal and dark basalt stones are illuminated softly by warm ivory parchment text and accents of aged guild brass, preventing the harsh optical fatigue common to high-contrast modern dark modes.

### Target Audience & Voice
Crafted for guild handlers, dispatchers, dragon-riders, and quartermasters operating in Bahasa Indonesia. The tone is formal, procedural, and dependable (*resmi, taktis, dan lugas*).

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for a deep dark-mode registry environment with a functional high-contrast structure.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#17191D`):** Deep volcanic charcoal providing an unobtrusive foundation.
- **Surface Body (`#202329`):** Guild registry stone surface used for cards, sidebars, and panel regions.
- **Surface Raised (`#282C33`):** Elevated stone tier for dropdowns, flyout menus, and modal dialogs.
- **Surface Muted (`#1C1F24`):** Recessed stone surface reserved for table headers, form inputs, and sunken wells.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#41464E`):** Chiseled stone edge delineating modular panels without soft blur.

### Accents & Typography Ink
- **Guild Gold (`#C5A15B`):** Antique guild brass used for primary call-to-actions, active navigation markers, and focus outlines.
- **Gold Hover (`#D5B873`):** Lifted metallic brass for interaction states.
- **Gold Ink (`#1A1916`):** Deep charcoal ink specifically for text and icons atop solid antique gold backgrounds to ensure WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Text Primary (`#F0EBDD`):** Warm ivory parchment avoiding the glare of stark white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Text Muted (`#B0B2B5`):** Balanced secondary stone gray for descriptors, labels, and table secondary content.
- **Text Subtle (`#898D93`):** Dimmed metadata for catalog codes and timestamps.

### Registry Status Semantics
- **Tersedia (Available):** Text `#6EB895` over surface `#20392F`. Denotes a dragon rested, fed, and cleared for flight.
- **Dipinjam (Borrowed):** Text `#D5A35B` over surface `#3C3020`. Signifies active mission dispatch or ongoing lease.
- **Terlambat / Bahaya (Overdue / Danger):** Text `#E17B79` over surface `#412527`. Highlights overdue return, combat injury, or critical system warning.
- **Informasi (Info):** Text `#8FB5D1` over surface `#242F39`. Neutral operational guild dispatches.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional duality: an antique guild serif for titles and identity anchors, coupled with an ultra-legible, crisp geometric sans-serif for operational administration.

### Type Pairings
- **Headings & Ledger Titles:** Set in **Libre Caslon Text**. Imparts an authentic scholarly, guild-registry pedigree reminiscent of ancient bestiaries and formal charters. Use exclusively for page titles, hero headers, modal titles, and dragon specimen names (`nama_unit`).
- **Body, Inputs, Tables & Controls:** Set in **Inter**. Delivers unyielding optical clarity across high-density inventory data, logs, dispatch calendars, and registry forms.

### Rules of Typesetting
- Avoid wide letter-spacing on regular text or paragraphs.
- All-caps styling is restricted strictly to compact metadata tags and system ledger codes (`kode_unit` e.g., `DRG-BALER-04`).
- Maintain a line-height multiplier between `1.4` and `1.6` for narrative text to ensure comfortable reading against dark stone backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a strict **4px/8px modular rhythm** with a maximum content canvas of `1440px`.

### Grid System & Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px – 1440px+):** 12-column grid with a fixed left administrative sidebar (`240px`). Catalog grids display 3 to 4 dragon units per row. Outer margins are fixed at `32px` (`margin-desktop`) with `24px` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. The admin sidebar collapses into a slide-over drawer triggered by a top-bar hamburger toggle. Catalog items flow into 2 columns. Tables gain horizontal smooth-scroll containers. Margin adjusts to `24px`.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column single-column stack. Forms display 1 input per row. Modals shift to bottom-sheet presentations. Margins tighten to `16px` (`margin`), with `16px` gutters.

### Component Spacing Principles
- **Touch Target Floor:** Desktop interactive targets must meet a minimum height of `40px`; mobile targets must meet `44px`.
- **Form Containment:** Creation and rental dispatch forms must not stretch past `720px` to maintain focused cognitive scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from diffused dropshadows, blurred glass, and floating glowing cards in favor of **tactile tonal layering and chiseled edges**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Foundation Level (0):** The app canvas `#17191D`. Recessed wells and table headers use `#1C1F24` with a 1px border `#41464E`.
2. **Surface Level (1):** Cards, primary containers, and sidebar modules sit on `#202329`, bounded by a sharp 1px border `#41464E`. No box-shadow is cast.
3. **Floating & Raised Tier (2):** Dropdown select panels, autocomplete suggestions, context menus, and modal dialogs occupy `#282C33`. To detach from underlying content, they use a crisp 1px `#41464E` border paired with an opaque ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Overlays
- **Modal Scrim:** Backdrop overlay is a flat `#0B0C0E` at `75%` opacity. Backdrop blur filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(...)`) are avoided to preserve the archival stone aesthetic.
- **Ornamental Brass Dividers:** High-level hero sections and modal headers may feature a restrained bottom hairline divider tinted with an antique brass gradient (`linear-gradient(90deg, #C5A15B 0%, #41464E 60%, transparent 100%)`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by **Soft (Level 1)** chiseling.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Micro-Controls:** Standard `4px` corner radius (`roundedness: 1`), evoking cut stone masonry and stamped metal tags.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers:** `6px` to `8px` corner radius (`rounded-lg`), grounding large registry blocks.
- **Modals & Drawers:** `8px` corner radius.
- **Restricted Pill Geometries:** Pill radii (`9999px`) are forbidden on standard action buttons, input fields, and panels. They are permitted solely on high-density status chips and category badges to maximize visual scanning differentiation against rectilinear data rows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Guild Gold):** Background `#C5A15B`, text color `#1A1916` (`font-weight: 600`), 1px border in `#D5B873`. Hover transitions to `#D5B873`. Active state shifts to `#B28F47`. Focus ring is a 2px offset outline of `#C5A15B`.
- **Secondary Action (Stone Surface):** Background `#202329`, text color `#F0EBDD`, border 1px solid `#41464E`. Hover shifts surface to `#282C33` with border `#C5A15B`.
- **Destructive Action:** Background `#412527`, text color `#E17B79`, border 1px solid `#E17B79`. Hover brightens surface to `#552E31`.

### Status Badges & Chips
Composed of an inline vector status dot or icon, followed by formal Indonesian status copy:
- **Tersedia:** Background `#20392F`, text `#6EB895`, border 1px solid `#2E5244`.
